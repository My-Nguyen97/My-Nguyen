# My-Nguyen

## About Me

Hello! My name is My Nguyen. I am currently pursuing my Master of Business Administration (MBA) at the Shidler College of Business at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa. I have a background in accounting, military leadership, and small business ownership. My goal is to continue growing professionally while learning how to expand my own business.

Before starting my business, I served in the United States Army as an E-5 Sergeant from 2016 to 2022. During my military service, I gained valuable experience in leadership, teamwork, problem-solving, and managing daily operations. The military taught me the importance of discipline, responsibility, and working with people from different backgrounds. These are skills that I continue to use in my everyday life and business.

I also studied accounting at the University of Hawaiʻi–West Oʻahu, which helped me develop an understanding of financial management and business operations.

Currently, I own and operate a small bánh mì business. I sell Vietnamese sandwiches at a farmers market every Saturday and also have a location in a food court. Running my business has been a valuable learning experience because I get to interact with customers, manage daily responsibilities, and understand the challenges of owning a small business.

One of my main reasons for pursuing an MBA is to gain more knowledge about business strategy, marketing, finance, and management. I want to learn how to make better business decisions, attract more customers, and improve the way my business operates.

My long-term goal is to open my own standalone bánh mì restaurant and eventually expand to multiple locations. I hope to combine my military experience, accounting education, and MBA knowledge to build a successful business that continues to grow while providing quality food and service to the community.
